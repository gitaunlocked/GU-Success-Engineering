Greetings from Gita Unlocked.
Gita Unlocked is a youth development initiative led by alumni, researchers and professionals from premier institutions including IITs, NITs, IIITs and leading global organizations. Over the past few years, we have conducted student development programmes across 20+ premier engineering institutions, engaging thousands of students through interactive sessions led by IIT alumni, researchers and industry leaders. Our initiatives are conducted as a student welfare effort without any commercial intent, with the objective of supporting students in their holistic development.
As students begin their college journey, they encounter not only exciting opportunities but also new challenges such as stress, distractions, peer pressure, unhealthy habits, and uncertainty about their future. Through our programmes, we endeavour to help students develop strong character, focus, discipline, resilience, and a balanced approach to academics and personal life.
We would be honoured to collaborate with IIIT Lucknow as part of the Orientation Programme 2026. We propose conducting our Orientation Session on the Induction Day. Thereafter, the six-session certificate programme may be conducted on any six days between 31st August and 10th September, subject to the availability of the students.
Proposed Programme
 
1. Orientation Session — "Unleash Your Inner Potential" (60 minutes)
An interactive session for all newly admitted students introducing practical insights on adapting to college life, building productive habits and preparing for long-term academic, personal and professional growth.
2. Certified Six-Session Student Development Programme (for shortlisted and interested students)
A structured follow-up programme of approximately 60 minutes per session, designed to complement students' academic journey and support their overall development.
The sessions will focus on
•  Personal Development and Self-Leadership
•  Character Building
•  Academic and Career Guidance
•  Developing Focus, Discipline and Productive Habits
•  Handling Challenges and Managing Stress
•  Decision-Making and Responsible Leadership
•  Communication and Overall Growth
The programme will be delivered through interactive discussions, practical activities and mentoring sessions facilitated by experienced professionals, IIT alumni and industry leaders.
